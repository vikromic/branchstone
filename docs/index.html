The Artist

I'm Viktoriia, originally from Ukraine and now creating in Santa Rosa, California. My work explores the intersection of nature and memory through mixed-media compositions using found materials—wood, moss, bark, and algae. Each piece tells a story of transformation, resilience, and the quiet beauty found in natural textures.

Working with materials shaped by time and elements, I seek to capture the essence of nature's impermanence and strength. My art is an invitation to slow down and observe the intricate details that often go unnoticed in our fast-paced world.
