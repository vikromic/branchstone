Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a commission take?

Commission timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the piece. Typically, a commissioned artwork takes 4-8 weeks from initial consultation to completion. I'll provide a more specific timeline during our initial discussion based on your requirements and my current workload.

What materials do you use?

I primarily work with high-quality acrylic paints on canvas or wood panels. I also incorporate mixed media techniques including texture pastes, metallics, and natural elements to create unique, layered compositions. All materials used are archival quality to ensure the longevity of your artwork.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, I ship artwork worldwide. All pieces are carefully packaged with professional-grade materials to ensure safe delivery. Shipping costs vary based on destination and artwork size. For international orders, please allow 2-4 weeks for delivery, and note that customs fees may apply based on your country's regulations.

How do I care for my artwork?

To keep your artwork looking its best, display it away from direct sunlight and moisture. Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid using cleaning products or water on the surface. If your piece is on canvas, avoid touching the painted surface directly. For additional protection, consider professional framing with UV-protective glass.

What is your refund policy?

For original artwork purchases, I offer a 7-day return policy from the date of delivery. The artwork must be returned in its original condition and packaging. For commissions, a 50% non-refundable deposit is required to begin work. I provide progress photos throughout the commission process to ensure your satisfaction. If you have concerns about your artwork, please contact me immediately so we can work together to find a solution.
