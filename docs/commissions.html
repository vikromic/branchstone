Frequently Asked Questions

Is the deposit refundable?

The $50 deposit is non-refundable once work begins, as it covers initial material sourcing and design time. However, if you need to cancel before I start working on your piece, the deposit can be refunded or applied to a future commission.

Can I see work in progress?

Absolutely! I share progress photos at key milestones throughout the creation process. You'll see your piece come to life and have opportunities to provide feedback during the journey.

What if I don't like the result?

Your satisfaction is important to me. We'll have thorough discussions and concept approval before I begin. During creation, I share progress updates so you can provide input. If there are concerns, we'll work together to address them. Minor adjustments can often be made to ensure you love your piece.

Do you take rush orders?

Rush orders may be possible depending on my current commission queue and the complexity of your project. Please mention your timeline when you inquire, and I'll do my best to accommodate special requests. Rush orders may include an additional fee to prioritize your project.

What materials do you use?

I work primarily with premium natural materials including wood, stone, natural fibers, and eco-friendly mediums. Each piece is unique, and materials are selected based on your vision and the specific requirements of your commission.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, I ship worldwide! Shipping costs vary by location and size of the artwork. International shipping is carefully coordinated to ensure your piece arrives safely, and I provide tracking information for all shipments.
