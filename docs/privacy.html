Site record / privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

This page describes what the current Branchstone website keeps in your browser, what happens when you choose to write an email, and the choices available to you.

Revised 18 July 2026
BRANCHSTONE06 NOTES

01

What stays on this device

The site can save a small amount of information in your browser’s local storage so that useful choices survive a page change or return visit:

  • theme preference (soil or paper)
  • language preference (English or Ukrainian)
  • the identifiers of works you save
  • a pending group inquiry assembled from saved works, kept for up to one hour
  • an unfinished commission draft, kept for up to 24 hours
This information remains on your device. The current site does not send it to a Branchstone server.

02

Email correspondence

Contact and commission actions prepare a message for your own email application using a mailto link. Branchstone does not receive the message unless you review it and choose to send it.

Once sent, your name, email address, message, and any details you include are handled by your email provider and received in the Branchstone inbox. They are used to reply, discuss an artwork, or continue a commission conversation.

03

No subscription or advertising profile

The current website does not offer a newsletter sign-up, build an advertising profile, or use advertising trackers. It does not sell personal information to advertisers.

A previous version of the site may have left older browser-local data on your device. Clearing this site’s browser data removes it.

04

Retention and your choices

Theme, language, and saved-work preferences remain until you change them or clear this site’s data. The pending inquiry and commission draft expire as described above.

You can clear browser-local information through your browser settings. For information contained in an email you sent, write to Branchstone to ask a question or request deletion where applicable.

05

External destinations

The site links to Instagram and to publications or exhibition pages. Those destinations operate under their own privacy practices. Following a link sends the usual request information to that external service.

06

Questions

For a privacy question, contact Branchstone directly:

thebranchstone@gmail.com
